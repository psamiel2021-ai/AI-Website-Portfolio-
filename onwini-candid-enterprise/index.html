Onwini Candid Enterprise is a retail business located in Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria. We specialise in the sale of electrical and electronic products, home appliances, generators, solar equipment, bedding, mattresses, furniture and other household essentials.

Our goal is to make it easier for individuals, families and businesses to access a variety of products they need for comfortable, functional and beautiful spaces.
